Honeymoons and couples travel

Your honeymoon should not feel like another trip you planned while exhausted. It is often the first time in months that nothing is on your list, and it deserves to feel like an exhale, with room to be fully present with each other.

The same approach works for anniversaries, milestone birthdays, and any couple who is overdue for time together. When life is full, quality time gets scheduled around everything else. A well-planned trip puts it first.

I plan around pace, privacy, and the moments that matter to the two of you:

  • The right resort for the mood you want, quiet and secluded or social and lively
  • A balance of rest and a few well-chosen experiences
  • Arrival and transfers planned so the trip begins smoothly
  • Romantic details arranged ahead of time
  • Unhurried mornings and room to do nothing

Sandals honeymoons

A truly all-inclusive honeymoon, so you can stop doing math.

Sandals is one of the few brands where all-inclusive really means all-inclusive. Once you arrive, the wallet stays in the room and the only decision is what to do next. For a honeymoon, that is the point.

  • Unlimited food, including up to 16 restaurants at some resorts, plus anytime snacks
  • Unlimited premium liquors and drinks at the bars, with no wristband tab to track
  • Tips, taxes, and gratuities covered, so there is no awkward cash handling all week
  • Golf with green fees included at the golf resorts in Jamaica and Saint Lucia
  • Scuba diving with PADI instructors for certified divers, plus water sports like snorkeling and kayaking
  • Round-trip airport transfers and entertainment day and night

Inclusions vary by resort and room category. Golf extras such as caddies and carts, spa treatments, candlelight dinners, and photography are not part of the package. I confirm exactly what your resort covers before you book.

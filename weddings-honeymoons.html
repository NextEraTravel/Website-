Destination weddings

Guest travel, resort coordination, timelines, contracts, family expectations. It adds up quickly, and most couples end up planning from a place of tired.

I work with destinations around the world, with a deep focus on Mexico and the Caribbean. Here is what I handle:

  • Resort and venue selection that fits your guest count, budget, and vision
  • Coordination with the resort wedding team
  • Group booking and room blocks for your guests
  • Guest travel logistics, so you are not answering the same question forty times
  • Timelines and details, tracked so nothing is left to chance

Your wedding should not feel like managing everyone else's travel. It should feel like your wedding.

Sandals weddings

Why I recommend Sandals for destination weddings.

I am a Sandals Wedding Specialist and a Sandals Group Specialist. I recommend the brand often for one reason: it takes the most stressful parts of planning a wedding away from home and puts them in experienced hands.

  • An on-property wedding team that knows the resort, the venues, and the process
  • All-inclusive stays, so your guests are not tracking costs all week
  • A range of Caribbean resorts, so the setting can match your style
  • Group booking support, which matters when guests are booking from different cities
  • A consistent standard of service, so you know what level of care to expect

I will tell you honestly when a Sandals resort is the right fit and when another property suits you better.

Honeymoons

Your honeymoon should not feel like another trip you planned while exhausted. It is often the first time in months that nothing is on your list, and it deserves to feel like an exhale.

I plan around pace, privacy, and the moments that matter to the two of you:

  • The right resort for the mood you want, quiet and secluded or social and lively
  • A balance of rest and a few well-chosen experiences
  • Arrival and transfers planned so the trip begins smoothly
  • Romantic details arranged ahead of time

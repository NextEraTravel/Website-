Planning investment

Most services begin with a planning investment, which compensates me for personalized research, comparison, and itinerary development. The amount is quoted to you before you commit, based on the scope of your trip. It is due before research begins and is non-refundable, because the work starts right away. The planning investment is separate from the cost of your travel services.

Some bookings, such as certain cruise-only reservations, may be handled without a planning investment. I will tell you which applies before we begin.

In addition to the planning investment, I may receive commissions or other compensation from travel suppliers when you book through me. I disclose this so you can make informed decisions. Changes and cancellations

Changes and cancellations are governed by each supplier's policies, which often include penalties or non-refundable amounts. I will explain the relevant policies before you book and help you with changes where possible. Supplier fees and penalties are your responsibility. The planning investment is not refundable if a trip is changed or cancelled.

You are responsible for having valid passports, visas, entry permits, vaccinations, and any other documents or requirements for every traveler.

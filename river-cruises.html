River cruise travel agent

A slower way to see the world.

On a river cruise the destination comes to you. You unpack once, the scenery changes outside your window, and each morning you step off in a new town. I plan it around how you like to travel.

Book a consultation

Why people love it

Smaller ships. A gentler pace.

River ships carry far fewer guests than ocean ships, so the experience feels personal. They dock close to the places you came to see, often within walking distance of the old town.

  • One unpack for the whole trip
  • Quiet evenings, with time to rest as well as explore
  • Many lines include excursions or drinks, which vary, so it is worth comparing

Personalized to you

The right line, ship, and cabin.

No two river lines feel the same. Some are lively and social, some are quiet and refined, some lean into food and wine. I match the ship and itinerary to you, and I tell you which cabin categories are worth it and which are not.

Everything around the sailing

The details that make it feel easy.

  • Hotel stays and transfers before and after your sailing
  • Flights that work with embarkation and disembarkation days
  • Coordination if friends or family are sailing together
  • Support before you go, so the details are settled before you leave

Not sure which to choose?

River or ocean

River suits you if

You want to explore towns and cities up close, prefer a quieter ship, and like a slower pace.

Ocean suits you if

You want more onboard variety, enjoy a livelier ship, or want to visit islands and coastlines across several ports.

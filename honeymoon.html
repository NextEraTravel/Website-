Most couples pick resorts backwards

Luxury is not the room. It is the feeling.

Most honeymoons start with photos: the prettiest pool, the nicest villa, the best deal. Then you arrive and find the resort is loud when you wanted quiet, or sleepy when you wanted energy. The wrong place changes the whole trip, so we start somewhere else.

Finish this sentence together: "After this trip, we want to feel ______." Rested. Connected. Adventurous. Celebrated. Then we choose the place that makes that feeling easy.

Private or social

A quiet villa with a plunge pool is a different honeymoon from a lively resort with a beach bar and a crowd.

Active or still

Water sports and excursions on one side, long mornings and a spa day on the other. Both are right for someone.

Dressed up or barefoot

Some resorts lean toward elegant dinners, and others are relaxed all day. Knowing your answer narrows the list fast.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them are open tabs.

Good to know

Questions couples ask first.

What happens on the call?

We talk through your dates, a rough budget range, and how you want to feel. I ask a few questions, tell you what I would consider and why, and explain how planning works. You leave with a clearer direction, whether or not we work together.

How much does it cost?

A planning fee may apply, based on the scope of your vacation. We talk through what that looks like on our call, before you decide anything. I charge a planning fee so my time goes into real research and personalized planning for you, not into a sales pitch.

Do you only book Sandals?

No. I am a Sandals Wedding Specialist and a Sandals Group Specialist, and I often recommend the brand for couples. It is not right for every couple, and I will tell you when it is not. My loyalty is to your honeymoon, not to a brand.

How far in advance should we book?

Several months ahead is a good rule, and longer for peak winter and holiday dates or a specific room category, because the most requested rooms sell out first. If your dates are closer than that, book a call anyway and I will tell you what is realistic.

Do you work with couples outside Florida?

Yes. I am based in Florida and work with couples all over the country. Our call happens live over video or phone, and everything after that is handled by email and message, so it works the same wherever you live.

Where do you plan honeymoons?

Mexico and the Caribbean are a deep focus for me, and I also plan custom honeymoons elsewhere. The destination follows from how you want to feel, so we start with the feeling.

Can you plan my wedding and honeymoon together?

Yes. If you are planning a destination wedding and a honeymoon, I plan them in the same conversation so the dates and the travel work together.

Why an advisor

The trip is the visible part. The judgment behind it is the value.

Anyone can find a resort online. Knowing which one fits you, which room is worth it, and what to ask before you commit is a different skill.

Fewer options, better ones

I narrow the field to the places that fit how you want to feel, so you are comparing a short list instead of forty tabs.

Details you would not think to ask

Room categories, resort layout, transfer times, what is included and what is not. Small details decide whether a trip feels easy or tiring.

One person to call

You work with me directly, from the first conversation through your return home. No call center and no booking queue.

Support if plans change

Flights shift, weather happens, guests change their minds. I stay involved so you are not sorting it out alone.

Who I work for

I work for you, not the resort or the vendor.

Your planning investment is how I am compensated for my time, research, and expertise. That is deliberate. It means my recommendations answer to you, not to whoever would like to fill a room.

Resorts and suppliers will always have a story to tell. My job is to tell you what fits, what does not, and what I would choose in your position. My loyalty is to my clients, not to vendors.

How it works

Planning that starts with a conversation.

Five clear steps, so you always know what happens next and what I need from you.

  1. 01

    We talk

    A live video consultation. I want to hear how you want the trip to feel, who is going, what has made planning hard so far, and what you already know you do not want. There is no script. You talk, I listen, and I ask the questions that matter.

  2. 02

    Your planning investment

    You make a planning investment, which covers personalized research and itinerary development. It is how I am paid for my work, so I can answer to you and not to a resort. Custom vacations start from $200 total. Weddings and group trips are quoted after we talk.

  3. 03

    Research and your plan

    I compare resorts, room categories, timing, and logistics, then bring you a focused plan with clear recommendations and the reasoning behind each one. You see what I chose, what I ruled out, and why.

  4. 04

    We refine and book

    We go through the plan together and adjust until it feels right. Once you decide, I book it and manage the details that come with it, including payment schedules, guest or group coordination, and confirmations.

  5. 05

    Support along the way

    Before you leave, I check that every detail is in place. While you are away, I am a message away if something changes. You travel knowing someone is looking out for you.

Good questions

Before you reach out

Why is there a planning investment?

Because the work starts long before a booking. Researching, comparing, and matching a trip to you takes time and judgment. The investment is what lets me work for you, not for a resort or vendor.

Do you work for the resorts?

No. I work for my clients. I partner with resorts and suppliers I trust, but my recommendations are built around you, and I will tell you when a property is not the right fit.

Could I just book this myself?

You can, and many people do. What I add is knowing which resort fits how you want to feel, which details change the trip, and having someone to call when something goes wrong.

How it works

Planning that starts with a conversation.

A clear process, so you always know what comes next.

  1. We talk

    We begin with a live video consultation. I want to hear how you want the trip to feel, who is going, and what has made planning hard so far.

  2. Planning fee

    The planning fee covers personalized research and itinerary development. It is non-refundable, and it is what allows me to give your trip my full attention.

  3. Your plan

    I research and present options matched to you, with clear recommendations and the reasoning behind them.

  4. We book

    Once you decide, I handle the booking and the details that come with it.

  5. Support along the way

    I stay in your corner before you leave and while you are away, so you are never on your own if something changes.

Questions people ask

Why do you charge a planning fee?

Because the work starts long before a booking. Researching, comparing, and matching a trip to you takes time and judgment. The fee means I can focus on your needs, not on whatever is quickest to sell.

Could I just book this myself?

You can, and many people do. What I add is knowing which resort fits how you want to feel, which room category is worth it, which details change the trip, and having someone to call when something goes wrong.

Where do you plan travel?

Around the world, with a particular focus on Mexico and the Caribbean for weddings and honeymoons.

How far ahead should I reach out?

The sooner the better, especially for destination weddings and group travel. If your trip is closer, reach out anyway and we will see what is possible.

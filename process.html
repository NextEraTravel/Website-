How it works

Planning that starts with a conversation.

A clear process, so you always know what comes next.

Every trip is different, but the path is the same. Here is what happens, what I need from you, and what you receive.

  1. We talk

    We begin with a live video consultation. This is a real conversation, not a form. I want to understand how you want the trip to feel before we talk about where it happens.

    • How you want to feel while you are away
    • Who is traveling and what each person needs
    • Dates, budget range, and anything that has made planning hard
    • Places or resorts you are already considering

  2. Your planning investment

    After our call, you make your planning investment and I begin the work. It covers personalized research and itinerary development, and it gives your trip my full attention.

    • Custom vacations start from $200 total
    • Destination weddings and group trips are quoted after we talk, based on scope
    • The investment is non-refundable, because the research begins right away

    Why it works this way

  3. Research and your custom plan

    I compare resorts, room categories, timing, transfers, and the experiences that suit you. Then I narrow it down to a focused plan instead of handing you a pile of options.

    • Clear recommendations with the reasoning behind each one
    • What I considered and ruled out, and why
    • Honest notes on trade-offs, so nothing surprises you later

  4. We refine it together

    We go through the plan on a call. You tell me what feels right and what does not, and I adjust. This is where a good plan becomes your plan.

    • Pace, room category, and the experiences worth including
    • Guest or group needs, if others are coming
    • Timing and what to book first

  5. We book

    Once you decide, I handle the booking and the details that come with it, so you are not tracking confirmations across a dozen emails.

    • Booking and confirmation of your travel
    • Payment schedules and deadlines, tracked for you
    • Guest booking and group coordination for weddings and group trips

  6. Support before and during your trip

    Before you leave, I confirm that every detail is in place. While you are away, I am available if something changes. You are not on your own.

    • A final check of arrangements before you travel
    • Someone to contact if flights, weather, or plans shift
    • A follow-up after you return, because I want to know how it felt

Your planning investment

I work for you, not the resort or the vendor.

Many travel advisors are paid only when you book, which can quietly shape what they recommend. I do it differently. Your planning investment compensates me for my work: the research, the comparison, the planning, and the attention.

That is what keeps my loyalty with you. I am free to say that a popular resort is not right for you, or that a simpler choice is the better one. I am loyal to my clients, not to vendors.

Questions people ask

Why is there a planning investment?

Because the work starts long before a booking. Researching, comparing, and matching a trip to you takes time and judgment. The investment means I can focus on your needs, not on whatever is quickest to sell.

Do you work for the resorts?

No. I work for my clients. I partner with resorts and suppliers I trust, but my loyalty is to you, and I will tell you plainly when a property is not the right fit.

What does the planning investment cover?

Personalized research, comparison, and itinerary development, plus the guidance that comes with them. It is non-refundable because the work begins as soon as we start.

Could I just book this myself?

You can, and many people do. What I add is knowing which resort fits how you want to feel, which room category is worth it, which details change the trip, and having someone to call when something goes wrong.

Where do you plan travel?

Around the world, with a particular focus on Mexico and the Caribbean for weddings and honeymoons.

How far ahead should I reach out?

The sooner the better, especially for destination weddings and group travel. If your trip is closer, reach out anyway and we will see what is possible.

Time away that brings you back to each other.

When life is full, quality time gets scheduled around everything else. A well-planned trip puts it first.

Less itinerary, more presence.

Many couples come to me after a demanding season. Work, family, and responsibilities have taken most of the space, and they want time that actually feels like theirs.

I plan these trips with that in mind. The pace is slower, the choices are fewer, and the details are arranged so the two of you can focus on each other.

  • Resorts chosen for privacy, atmosphere, and quality of service
  • Unhurried mornings and room to do nothing
  • A small number of curated experiences instead of a packed schedule
  • Dinners, spa time, and special touches arranged in advance

Who this is for

Couples marking an anniversary or milestone. Partners who are overdue for time together. Two people who want a trip that restores them, not one that adds another list to manage.

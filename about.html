About

Hi, I am Christine.

I plan travel the way I wish more people were planned for: personally, carefully, and with real attention to how the trip will feel.

My story

I grew up in the Bronx and found my real work later.

I moved to Florida in 2016. For almost fifteen years I worked in healthcare administration, starting at the bottom and working my way into management. I was good at it. I learned how to keep complicated things running and how to care for people who were having a hard time.

Somewhere in that role I realized I was meant for more. Around the same time I started traveling, and it became clear that travel was central to the life I wanted. It was not about the destinations alone. It was about what a well-planned trip does for a person.

Why Next Era Travel

I built this around one idea: the trip should feel as good as the photos.

I work as a concierge-style advisor, which means you speak with me, not a call center or a booking site. I take on a limited number of clients so each one gets real attention, and I lean on my training and supplier relationships to shape trips you could not easily build yourself.

The name matters to me. Next Era is about the chapter that comes next, whether that is a marriage, a rest you have earned, or the first trip you have taken for yourself in years.

How I work

What you can expect from me

Personal

You work with me directly, from your first consultation to your return home. I know your trip and I know you.

Selective

I keep my client list limited, so each trip gets the time it deserves and nothing is rushed.

Honest

If a resort is not right for you, I will say so. My recommendations come from fit, not from what is easiest to sell.

Loyal to you

I work for my clients, not for the resort or the vendor. Your planning investment is what makes that possible.

What I believe

Good planning is not about finding more options. It is about narrowing them to the right one.

That is the work. I listen closely, filter out the noise, and recommend with confidence, so you can stop researching and start looking forward to it.

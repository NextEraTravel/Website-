About

Hi, I am Christine.

I plan travel the way I wish more people were planned for: personally, carefully, and with real attention to how the trip will feel.

I grew up in the Bronx and moved to Florida in 2016. For almost fifteen years I worked in healthcare administration, starting at the bottom and working my way into management.

Somewhere in that role I realized I was meant for more. Around the same time I started traveling, and it became clear that travel was central to the life I wanted. It was not about the destinations alone. It was about what a well-planned trip does for a person.

I built Next Era Travel around that idea. I work as a concierge-style advisor, which means you speak with me, not a call center or a booking site. I take on a limited number of clients so each one gets real attention, and I lean on my training and supplier relationships to shape trips you could not easily build yourself.

I am a Virtuoso-affiliated advisor and a Sandals Certified Specialist, based in Ocala, Florida, and I plan trips around the world with a particular focus on Mexico and the Caribbean.

What I believe

Good planning is not about finding more options. It is about narrowing them to the right one.

That is the work. I listen closely, filter out the noise, and recommend with confidence, so you can stop researching and start looking forward to it.

Custom luxury vacations

A trip built around the people who are going.

No packages and no template itineraries. I plan custom vacations for couples, families, and multigenerational groups, starting from a conversation about how you want the trip to feel.

Book a consultation

Planned around you, not around a package.

A custom trip starts with a few honest questions. Who is coming, what pace suits everyone, and what would make the week feel like it was worth it. From there I research, narrow the options, and build the plan, so you are choosing between a few right answers instead of hundreds of tabs.

  • Resorts, rooms, and experiences matched to your group and your pace
  • Flights, transfers, and timing arranged so arrival is easy
  • Dining, activities, and special touches planned before you land
  • One person to call if anything changes while you are away

Who custom travel is for

Couples. Anniversaries, milestone birthdays, and long overdue time together, planned at a slower pace so you can reconnect without a schedule.

Families. Trips where the kids stay happy and the adults actually get to rest, with the logistics handled in advance.

Multigenerational groups. Grandparents, parents, and children on one trip, with something that works for every age and energy level.

Custom vacations are for places around the world, from Caribbean and Mexico resorts to Europe, the Maldives, and beyond. If you have tried planning it yourself and ended up with forty open tabs and no decision, this is a good fit.

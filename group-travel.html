One point of contact, start to finish.

Group travel multiplies every detail. More people, more questions, more moving parts. I act as your single point of contact so the logistics stay organized and you stay present for your group.

  • Destination and resort selection that fits your group and your goals
  • Group rates and room blocks
  • Booking coordination for your attendees
  • Logistics planned in advance, from arrivals to schedules
  • A clear plan, so you are not answering the same questions repeatedly

Two ways to travel

Escorted or independent

Both are available. We will decide which one fits your group during the consultation.

Escorted groups

A tour director or host travels with your group and manages the flow of each day. A good fit for larger groups, first-time visitors to a destination, or anyone who wants less to coordinate on the ground.

Independent groups

Your group travels on its own schedule while I coordinate the bookings, room blocks, and logistics ahead of time. A good fit when you want flexibility and a custom itinerary.

Retreat hosts

Hosting a retreat? Let the travel be the easy part.

When you host a retreat, you are holding the vision for your people and the numbers at the same time. I take on the travel side, so your setting supports your work and the whole experience looks as professional as you are.

  • A destination and venue that fit your price point, your people, and your program
  • A realistic per-person cost, so the retreat can work financially before you announce it
  • Room blocks, attendee bookings, and deadlines tracked in one place
  • Contracts and cancellation terms read and explained before you sign
  • A clear plan for arrivals, transfers, and schedules, so nobody is left wondering

I do not run your program. I handle the travel around it, so you can focus on the people in the room.

Two ways to travel

Escorted or independent

Both are available. We will decide which one fits your group during the consultation.

Escorted groups

A tour director or host travels with your group and manages the flow of each day. A good fit for larger groups, first-time visitors to a destination, or anyone who wants less to coordinate on the ground.

Independent groups

Your group travels on its own schedule while I coordinate the bookings, room blocks, and logistics ahead of time. A good fit when you want flexibility and a custom itinerary.
